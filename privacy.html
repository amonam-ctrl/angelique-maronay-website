A NOTE ON PRIVACY

Privacy note

This privacy note explains how information submitted through the website forms is used. Please reach out through the contact form with any questions.

What we collect

If you complete the contact form, you provide your name, email address, and message. If you sign up for the reader-interest list, you provide your email address and your consent to updates.

How we use it

Contact form information is used to respond to inquiries. Reader-interest information is collected so you may be contacted with author or book news after a mailing service is configured. Signing up does not immediately start an automated newsletter.

Where information goes

The forms are handled by Netlify Forms, so submissions are processed and retained by Netlify in the website administrator's account. Additional email services will be described here if one is connected in the future.

Your choices

Contact the website owner through the contact form to request a copy of, correction to, or removal of the information you submitted. If email updates begin, each newsletter will provide an unsubscribe method.

Updates

This note may be updated as the website changes or other services are added. Last prepared: October 21, 2026.
